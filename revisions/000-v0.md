# M_0 → M_1 — spec v0 and four implementations

**Date:** 2026-08-02
**Spec version:** v0

$$\mathcal{M}_t = \text{best current organization of perspectives, limits, and possibilities}$$

## What changed

The project moved from a reading of the source text to a specification with four
implementations answerable to it.

- `docs/spec/core.md` — a normative, language-independent definition of a
  generative field, with every choice the source text leaves open marked
  `[OPEN → CHOICE]` and pointed at a contradiction record.
- `conformance/vectors/` — twelve shared vectors. Each carries hand-written
  expectations derived from the spec, and a digest generated from the reference
  implementation.
- `impl/python` — reference implementation. 30 tests.
- `impl/typescript` — 12 tests, agrees digest-for-digest with the reference.
- `impl/julia` — 67 test assertions, agrees digest-for-digest with the reference.
- `impl/lean` — formalisation. Proves the absorption theorem for all fields,
  makes principle 2 hold by typing rather than convention, and refutes one of
  our own requirements.
- `contradictions/` — four entries, three from the source text and one from us.
- `tools/lint_principles.py` — the seven principles enforced on the repository.

## What was lost

Stating this is required by principle 7. It is not a formality.

**A claim we thought we had.** The spec asserted `Loss(F) > 0` for translations
between ontologies. The Lean formalisation proved this false of our measure:
integer division floors a small residue against a large image to zero. Three
implementations and twelve vectors agreed with each other and were wrong
together. The assertion in §8 is now scoped to the built-in pairs at corpus
scale, and the defect is open as
[`contradictions/004`](../contradictions/004-loss-can-round-to-zero.md).

**Generality, in exchange for determinism.** Everything is integer fixed-point
with no division, and the possibility space is a bounded canonical enumeration.
Four implementations can therefore be compared bit-for-bit — but "accessible"
now means *accessible among enumerated*, which is narrower than §3 intends.

**Higher cells above the 2-cell.** §6 specifies `α : r ⇒ r'` and
`β : α ⇛ α'`. Relation rewriting gives the first. `β` is specified and not
implemented. Whether the tower terminates is not addressed.

**Arbitrary constants.** `THETA`, `TAU`, `WINDOW`, `PERSISTENCE`, `COHESION`
and the eight rule weights are chosen, not derived. `contradictions/002` records
the reasoning for two of them; the rest have none beyond "these produce legible
behaviour at corpus scale".

**Domain grounding.** By decision, the core is abstract. Every illustration in
the source text — cell, stem cell, ecosystem, diagnosis, child — remains
unmodelled. Nothing here has been shown to say anything true about any of them.

## What this does not claim

That the spec is right, that the four implementations agreeing makes them
correct (004 is the standing counterexample), or that levels 5 and 6 of the
ladder are reached. Level 4 has runnable, checked content. Level 5 has partial
content. Level 6 is not a target.
